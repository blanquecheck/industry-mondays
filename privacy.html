Privacy

The Industry Mixer runs Industry Mondays. This notice covers what we collect when you RSVP on this site and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Anything optional you add: Instagram handle, what you do and where you work.
  • Whether you chose email or text updates, and the time you RSVP'd.
  • The link that brought you here (for example a referral code), so we know which promoters and posts work.

What we use it for

  • Putting you on the RSVP list for free happy hour.
  • Sending you updates about upcoming nights, only if you ticked the email or text box.
  • Understanding who comes, so we can plan better nights.

We never sell your details. They are stored in a private Google Sheet that only the Industry Mondays team can open.

Your choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message to stop them, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link in any email.
